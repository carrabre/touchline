# Instrumental soundtrack pool

Touchline chooses Hunted, Legionnaire (Original), or Titan at random for each new reel revision. Regenerating in random mode avoids the preceding track; retrying the same revision keeps its chosen track. Specific tracks can also be selected in the studio. Match audio is retained, with normalized music ducked beneath speech and faded at both ends.

All three tracks are by Scott Buckley, released under CC BY 4.0. Official MP3 downloads are bundled in the media worker, rather than fetched from YouTube during rendering. The catalogue records source URLs, YouTube originals, license URLs and SHA-256 checksums. Instrumentation is orchestral; no sung lyrics are used.

- Hunted: https://www.scottbuckley.com.au/library/hunted/ — https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=W_Q9x60xLEg
- Legionnaire (Original): https://www.scottbuckley.com.au/library/legionnaire/ — https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7uIYYEM45b4
- Titan: https://www.scottbuckley.com.au/library/titan/ — https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vtu8f2dKriY

License: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/
Composer usage instructions: https://www.scottbuckley.com.au/library/using-this-music/

Each MP4 embeds attribution in its comment metadata. The worker also writes a credits text file, and the authenticated studio offers Download music credits. Users must include these credits in a YouTube video description when publishing; embedded metadata alone does not satisfy the composer's YouTube description requirement. The credits disclose trimming, looping, fading and mixing.

The older original Sunday Rise synthesizer remains in source history and legacy reels under CC0; it is no longer in the random pool.
