# Sunday Rise

Original instrumental composed and synthesized specifically for Touchline in `worker/music.py`. D-major four-chord progression, bass, rising arpeggios and pulse at 108 BPM. No third-party recordings, samples, melodies, or music services are used.

The composition, generated recording and synthesizer source are dedicated to the public domain under **CC0 1.0 Universal**: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

No attribution is required. The source is included in the repository so the music can be inspected or regenerated. The 32-second phrase loops under the reel, with fades and sidechain ducking against original game audio. Rights to the uploaded source footage remain with its owner.
